Code is read far more often than it is written. Writing code with high maintainability is fundamentally an exercise in empathy for future readers—including our future selves.

## The Illusion of Cleverness

Clever one-liners frequently trade comprehension for brevity. Quality software prioritizes clear naming, obvious control flow, and mechanical sympathy over syntactic tricks.
