Today, I asked ChatGPT to review my career. I talked about a recent project, and it asked me follow up questions based on my answers. It then summarized my strenghs and weaknesses.

I was surprised because the results felt close to how I see myself. Of course, this reviews was based on our conversation, not on a direct review of my code or work. Still it gave me a chance to reflect on where I am today.

# Strengths and Weaknesses

## Strengths

- I understand what people need and turn those needs into useful featrues.
- I have experience using LLMs to automate repetitive tasks and improve efficiency at work.
- I think about which techonologies are needed to solve a problem and try to make complex systems simpler.

## Weaknesses

- I don't have a clear system for evaluating LLM quality.
- When i change a prompt or pipeline, it is hard to see exactly what has changed compared to before.
- I don't have enough data to decide which approach is better when considering quality, cost, and processing time together.

# What's Next

I plan to create evaluation data and clear criteria based on real work. Then, I want to compare the results before and after changing prompts, models or pipelines.

My goal is not simply to get higher quality scores. I want to use evaluation results to choose an approach that fits the work while considering both quality and cost.

I want to build on my experience with LLMs by learning how to evaluate and test them better, so I can make better decisions as a developer.
